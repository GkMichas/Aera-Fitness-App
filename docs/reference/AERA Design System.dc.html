AERA Design System · V1
Adapted from AERA_Design_System_V1.md

Understand your body. Become your best.

A premium consumer health product — not a gym dashboard, not a calorie spreadsheet. Clarity and data carry the interface; photography and motion carry the feeling.

Balance 70% clarity + data 30% emotional storytelling
Feels like Calm confidence Progress, strength, intelligence
Never Neon gym, hospital No gradients, no gamification
Voice Coach, not cheerleader Direct, practical, never shaming

10

How the spec maps to this system

AERA_Complete_Application_Spec_V1 · 5 primary destinations, 20+ screens
Home Greeting · status strip of 5 metrics · Insight · Today's Plan (training + nutrition) · check-in entry MetricCard, InsightCard, PlanCard
Coach One calm conversation with contextual action cards — the only place the AI is visible as itself CoachMessage, ActionCard
Training Dashboard → workout detail → exercise detail → active session → library. Video-forward. WorkoutCard, ExerciseCard, VideoPoster
Nutrition Targets dashboard, meal detail, text meal logging, planner, food search. Photography-forward. MealCard, MediaCard
You Body, measurements, progress charts, photos, goals — plus Weekly Review and AERA Health ChartCard, ProgressRing, TrendIndicator
The product loop Collect → Understand → Plan → Execute → Measure → Learn → Adapt Every screen belongs to one step of the loop. If a screen doesn't advance it, it doesn't ship in V1.
Safety surface Estimates are labelled. Health is never a diagnosis. Body composition and parsed meals carry an “Estimated” badge. AERA Health uses structured follow-ups and escalates to professional care.
Every flow needs Loading · Skeleton · Empty · Error · Retry Empty states coach rather than apologise: “Complete your first workout and AERA will start learning your training patterns.”
