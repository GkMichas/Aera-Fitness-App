## Additional production screens required beyond the 31

The audit identified several screens that are required by the master application specification even though they were not fully represented in the 31-screen visual set:

- Exercise Library with filters/search
- Food Search
- Meal Detail
- Complete Body Dashboard
- Progress Photos comparison
- Settings
- Notification Preferences
- Privacy / data controls
- Account deletion/export flow
- Subscription management

These are part of the production plan and must use the same design system.
